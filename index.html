How to use CivicPulse

  1. Report: describe the problem by typing or tapping "Speak instead". Add a photo if you have one.
  2. Pin it: tap the map or press "Use my location", then submit. You get a complaint ID, the priority and the department, with the reason.
  3. Duplicates: if someone already reported the same problem within 100 m, your report joins that case.
  4. Map: see every case on the Map tab. Turn on "Show hotspots" to see where problems cluster.
  5. Dashboard: see totals, active and resolved cases, high-priority issues and category charts.
  6. Track: enter your ID to see Reported, Assigned, In Progress or Resolved.
  7. Department: change a status here and watch it update on the Track page.
